# 4. Cart

Customer should be able to:

```text
Add product
Remove product
Increase quantity
Decrease quantity
View cart
Clear cart
```

Important:

Do NOT blindly trust prices or product information sent by the frontend.

The backend should retrieve the current product information from the database and calculate important values itself.

For example:

```text
Frontend:
productId = 123
quantity = 2
price = 999
```

The backend should NOT simply trust:

```text
price = 999
```

Instead:

```text
Backend
    ↓
Find product in MongoDB
    ↓
Get current price
    ↓
Validate stock
    ↓
Calculate total
```

# 7. Inventory

When an order is successfully created:

Example:

```text
Product stock = 10

Customer purchases = 2

New stock = 8
```

Do not allow:

```text
Stock = 2
Purchase = 5

→ Reject order
```

The backend must prevent negative stock.

Also consider the possibility of two customers attempting to purchase the same product at nearly the same time.

The inventory logic should be designed to minimize/prevent overselling.

This should be implemented in a way that can be explained during an interview.

# INTERVIEW OBJECTIVE

This is not just a coding project.

I want to be able to explain it in a full-stack interview.

After completing the project, I should be able to answer questions such as:

### Architecture

* Why did you choose Node.js/Express?
* Why MongoDB?
* How is your backend structured?
* Why did you separate controllers and services?
* How does a request flow through your application?

### Authentication

* How does login work?
* How are passwords stored?
* How do you protect routes?
* How does authorization work?
* Why HttpOnly cookies?
* What happens if a user's token is invalid?

### Database

* How are your collections related?
* Why did you use references?
* How does `populate()` work?
* How do you search products?
* How does pagination work?
* Where would you use indexes?

### E-commerce

* How does checkout work?
* How do you calculate the final price?
* How do you prevent users from manipulating prices?
* How do you prevent ordering out-of-stock products?
* How do you prevent overselling?
* How do reviews verify that a customer purchased the product?

### Security

* How do you prevent unauthorized access?
* How do you protect passwords?
* What is CORS?
* What is XSS?
* What is CSRF?
* How do you protect against NoSQL injection?

### Scalability

* What would become a bottleneck if the application had 100,000 users?
* Where would you introduce caching?
* What database queries would you optimize?
* How would you handle large product catalogs?

I should understand the answers rather than memorize them.
